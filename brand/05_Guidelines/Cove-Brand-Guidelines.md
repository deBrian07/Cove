# Cove — Brand Guidelines

## Identity
**Cove** is a modern native Chromium browser. The visual identity uses a wave-shaped **C**: two layered ribbons creating a protected cove / open horizon. The identity should feel calm, confident, and clear rather than heavily decorated.

**Tagline:** A calmer way to browse.

## Approved colors
| Token | Hex | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Ocean | #2563EB | Accent buttons, active states, links |
| Slate | #0F172A | Primary text, dark icon backgrounds |
| Mist | #F1F5F9 | Light panels and soft fills |
| Cloud | #E2E8F0 | Dividers, borders |
| Sand | #F8FAFC | Light canvases and surfaces |
| White | #FFFFFF | Content surfaces and inverse text |

The icon mark uses a cool blue gradient (upper wave) and a darker blue-to-slate gradient (lower wave). Do not replace it with the nested-C logo from the earlier design rounds.

## Typography
Use **Inter Display** for marketing headlines and product wordmark; **Inter** or platform-native sans-serif for interface text. We do not redistribute typeface files in this brand package. Recommended wordmark: Inter Display SemiBold; body/UI text: Inter Regular/Medium.

## Logo files
- **01_Logos/**: transparent SVG and PNG exports for horizontal, stacked, gradient and monochrome variants.
- On light backgrounds, use `Cove-Horizontal-OnLight.svg`.
- On dark backgrounds, use `Cove-Horizontal-OnDark.svg`.
- White monochrome is suitable for 1-color and reversed placements.
- `Cove-Symbol-Gradient.svg` is the independent emblem without text.

## App icons and favicons
- **02_App_Icons/**: macOS light and dark icon variants at 16 through 1024 px, source SVG, `.icns`, and an `.iconset` directory.
- **03_Favicons/**: transparent wave mark from 16 px to 512 px, plus multi-resolution `favicon.ico`.
- Prefer the full app icon in macOS Dock, and the bare wave mark for favicons.

## Clear space, minimum sizes, and contrast
- Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the emblem height on all sides of the logo.
- Horizontal wordmark: minimum 120 px wide for crisp text.
- Independent symbol: minimum 20 px on modern screens; for 16 px, use the dedicated favicon export.
- Keep sufficient contrast, especially for blue controls on light gray surfaces.
- Avoid recoloring the two-layer gradient without checking the entire logo system.

## Copy & tone
- Product: **Cove** (initial capital only)
- Tagline: **A calmer way to browse.**
- Style: quiet, straightforward, specific. Avoid clutter and exaggerated claims.

## Production note
The approved direction was a rendered design-board image, not original editable vector artwork. The emblem SVGs here are carefully traced vector recreations of the wave-shaped C in that image. They are practical source assets, but may have minute differences from a hypothetical original vector master. The marketing art is illustrative, not a screenshot of the current native application. Before release, review assets at actual display sizes.
